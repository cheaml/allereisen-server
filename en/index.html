Your trips, from your photos.

AlleReisen turns the photos and videos of a trip into a travel journal: a world map, stops with routes, days with your own notes, and a slideshow.

  • Photos from your photo library, from folders on your device or from OneDrive. Your originals stay where they are – they are neither changed nor uploaded.
  • Works without an account. No ads, no tracking.
  • Optionally connect your own server to share trips with family and friends. The server code is free software under the AGPL-3.0.
  • For iPhone, iPad and Windows.

AlleReisen is free

If you like the app, we would be glad if you made a voluntary donation to one of these organisations in Vienna – directly on their website:

  • Die Gruft – Caritas shelter for homeless people
  • St. Anna Kinderspital – children's hospital

Donations go directly to the organisation. The provider of AlleReisen receives no money and no data about them.
