Your own AlleReisen website

The app works fully without a website. An own website is an extra – for everyone who wants to share trips with family and friends or edit them on several devices.

Someone in your family already runs one? Then there is nothing to set up. Ask the person who runs it to add you as a member, and connect in the app under Settings → Sign-in & connections → “Connect to my website” with its address.

What does an own website add?

  • Sharing: family and friends see your trips in the browser or in their app – guests too, with an invitation link.
  • Several devices: one trip, editable on iPhone, iPad and in the browser; changes sync by themselves.
  • Backup: texts, stops and routes are also kept on your website, with a backup before each change.
  • Trip automation (optional): new photos in OneDrive can become trips by themselves.

Where do my photos stay?

Your original photos and videos stay in OneDrive. The website only stores texts, stops, routes and small preview images. The website is yours: it runs on your own web hosting, not with us. We run no servers for users and do not see your data.

What do I need?

  • Web hosting with PHP and MySQL/MariaDB – most common hosting packages offer this (PHP 8.3 or newer, MySQL 8 or MariaDB 10.11 or newer).
  • An own address with HTTPS, for example trips.your-domain.com. Many providers include the certificate for free.
  • A Microsoft account and a free app registration with Microsoft. Everyone signs in through it, and the website may read the photos in OneDrive. The guide explains every step.
  • Some experience with uploading files and creating a database (for example with phpMyAdmin in your hosting control panel). If this is new to you, ask someone among family or friends who looks after websites.

What does it cost, how long does it take?

AlleReisen itself is free and open source. You only pay for your web hosting – depending on the provider a few euros a month; often the website also fits into an existing package. Plan one to two hours for the setup.

The steps in short

  1. Create an address (subdomain with HTTPS) and an empty database with your hosting provider.
  2. Download the server package from GitHub and upload the files.
  3. Import the database template and fill in the settings file (database, address, your e-mail as administrator).
  4. Register an app with Microsoft and enter its ID in the settings file.
  5. Sign in in the browser, import a small test trip – done.

Then connect the app: Settings → Sign-in & connections → “Connect to my website”, enter the address, sign in with Microsoft.
