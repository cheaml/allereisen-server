Privacy policy

For the AlleReisen apps for iPhone, iPad and Windows. Last updated: November 2026. In case of doubt, the German version applies.

In short

  • AlleReisen works without an account. Your trips are stored on your device.
  • The provider of the app does not collect personal data: no advertising, no tracking, no usage analytics, no crash reports sent to the provider.
  • Your original photos and videos stay where they are (photo library, folders, OneDrive). The app neither changes nor moves them.
  • Data leaves your device only for features you use yourself: Microsoft sign-in and OneDrive, map display, weather (if switched on) and a server you connect.

2. Data on your device

For your trips the app stores on your device: titles, notes, participant names, stops with place and time, routes, references to the original media and small preview images. This data stays on the device unless you delete it or synchronise it with a server. You can delete trips in the app at any time; deleting the app removes all of this data.

Photos and videos

Only with your permission does the app read media from the albums, folders or OneDrive folders you choose, including capture time and location (GPS), to create stops and days. Originals are not changed and not transmitted to the provider.

Apple Health (optional, iPhone only)

Only if you explicitly allow it, the app reads your daily step counts for travel days on which you are listed as a participant and stores them as daily values with those trips. The app does not write anything to Apple Health. If you synchronise trips with your own server, these values are transmitted there and visible to people allowed to view the trip. Health data is never used for advertising and never shared with third parties. You can remove the imported values in the app at any time. For this reason the trip catalogue is excluded from iCloud device backups.

Backup file

On request the app creates a backup file of your trip catalogue at a location you choose. It contains notes and references to media, but no original files, no credentials and no Health values.

3. Microsoft sign-in and OneDrive (optional)

When you connect OneDrive, you sign in directly with Microsoft. The app receives permission to read your files; credentials are kept in your device's protected storage (e.g. Keychain). Media and previews are loaded directly from Microsoft. The Microsoft privacy statement applies. You can disconnect in the app at any time and additionally revoke the permission at account.live.com/consent/Manage.

4. Maps and place names

To display maps, map tiles are loaded. The providers technically receive your IP address and the displayed map area (but not your photos):

  • iPhone and iPad: Apple Maps (Apple privacy); some maps additionally use satellite imagery from Esri.
  • Windows: satellite imagery from Esri (Esri privacy).
  • Names of coastal places and waters for the displayed area are requested from the OpenStreetMap service Overpass (OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy).

5. Weather of travel days (optional, off by default)

If you switch on weather, the app sends the date and GPS position of each displayed travel day to Open-Meteo to retrieve the daily values (Open-Meteo terms).

6. Own server (optional)

You can connect the app to a self-hosted AlleReisen website to share trips and synchronise between devices. Trip data (titles, notes, stops, participants, media references, preview images and, if used, step values) is then transmitted to that server. The operator of a server is responsible for the data on it, not the provider of the app. The provider does not operate a general server for users of the app.

Deleting an account on a server

You can delete a server account in the app under “Anmeldung & Verbindungen” (sign-in & connections) → “Eigener Server” → “Serverkonto löschen”, or on the website under “Anmeldung & Verbindungen”. This deletes your account, your own trips with all stops and notes, your connections and the access granted to you on the server. You decide yourself whether the trips and preview images on your device are deleted as well. Backups made by a server operator may persist until their retention period ends.

7. Sharing

The provider does not sell or share data. Transfers to the services named above only happen when you use the respective feature and only to the extent necessary.

8. Your rights

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection. As the provider does not collect data about you, these rights usually concern the services named above or the operator of your server. For questions write to hilfe@fairhandeln.at. You may also lodge a complaint with the Austrian Data Protection Authority, Barichgasse 40–42, 1030 Vienna, or the supervisory authority of your country.

9. Changes

If features of the app that affect data change, this policy will be updated. The version published here applies.
