The app's user interface is currently in German.

Getting started

Create a trip

Choose “Reise importieren” (import trip) and pick a source: an album in your photo library, a folder on your device or a OneDrive folder. AlleReisen creates days and stops from capture time and location. The originals stay unchanged where they are.

Edit notes and stops

Within a trip you can write daily notes, name, merge or move stops and add participants.

Connect OneDrive

Sign in with your Microsoft account under “Anmeldung & Verbindungen” (sign-in & connections). Sign-in happens directly with Microsoft; you can disconnect at any time in the same place.

Share trips with your own server

With a self-hosted AlleReisen website you can share trips with family and friends and synchronise between devices. Installation guide and code: github.com/cheaml/allereisen-server. In the app, enter the address under “Anmeldung & Verbindungen” → “Eigener Server” and connect.

Delete an account

Without a server there is no account: delete trips directly in the app; all data is removed when you delete the app. A server account is deleted in the app under “Anmeldung & Verbindungen” → “Eigener Server” → “Serverkonto löschen”. What is deleted is described in the privacy policy.
