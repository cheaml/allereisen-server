Help & contact

Contact: hilfe@fairhandeln.at (English or German)

Please include the app version, device and operating system, the trip concerned and exactly what was shown. Do not send passwords, sign-in codes or private original photos.

The app is available in English and German. It follows the device language; you can also switch it under “Account” → “Language”.

Getting started

Create a trip

Tap “Import” on the world map and pick a source: an album in your photo library, a folder on your device or a OneDrive folder. AlleReisen creates days and stops from capture time and location. The originals stay unchanged where they are.

Edit notes and stops

Within a trip you can write daily notes, name, merge or move stops and add participants.

Connect OneDrive

Sign in with your Microsoft account under “Account” → “OneDrive”. Sign-in happens directly with Microsoft; you can disconnect at any time in the same place.

Share trips with your own server

Optionally, you can run your own AlleReisen website to share trips with family and friends and to synchronise between devices. The server runs on your own (or your family’s) web hosting – the provider of the app does not operate a server for users. The server is free open-source software; installation guide and code: github.com/cheaml/allereisen-server. In the app, enter your website’s address under “Account” → “Own server” and connect.

Delete an account

Without a server there is no account: delete trips directly in the app; all data is removed when you delete the app. A server account is deleted in the app under “Account” → “Own server” → “Delete server account”. What is deleted is described in the privacy policy.

Donations

AlleReisen is free. If you like the app, we would be glad if you donated to Die Gruft (Caritas Vienna) or St. Anna Kinderspital – directly on their websites.
